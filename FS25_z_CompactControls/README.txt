Compact Controls 1.0.0.0 for Farming Simulator 25

INSTALL: Copy FS25_CompactControls.zip into your FS25 mods folder, then enable
Compact Controls when loading your save. Keep the ZIP intact.

The standard upper-left F1 help panel is 15% smaller. Text, buttons, spacing,
backgrounds and its vehicle diagram are reduced together. Its top-left anchor
stays fixed. Other independently drawn mod HUD panels retain their own sizes.

ADJUST: Inside CompactControls.lua change SCALE = 0.85 to 0.80 for 20% smaller,
or 0.75 for 25% smaller, then repackage the files at the ZIP root.

Built against the documented FS25 InputHelpDisplay sizing routine, with
modDesc version 114 matching the installed FS25 1.24.0.0 log.
In-game validation is still required, especially with replacement help-menu mods.

REMOVE: Disable Compact Controls or remove its ZIP. No savegame data is changed.
