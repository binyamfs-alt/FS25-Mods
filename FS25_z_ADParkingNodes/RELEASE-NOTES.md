# AutoDrive Parking Node Rows 1.0.0.6

Lay out a straight row of AutoDrive parking nodes with two clicks, instead of
dragging each node into alignment.

- Find **AutoDrive Parking Node Row** under **Construction > Decorations > Others**.
- Click a starting point, stretch the preview, then click to place the row.
- Choose primary or secondary nodes with **B**.
- Adjust maximum spacing from **1 to 10 m** with **N / M**; default 5 m.
- Preview shows row length and node count. Both endpoints are included, with
  equal gaps no larger than the selected maximum. Rows can be up to 500 m.
- Nodes are unconnected. No physical decoration, landscaping change or fee.

Requires **FS25_AutoDrive**. Keep the original AutoDrive mod installed and enable
both mods. Save normally to retain the new nodes in AutoDrive's configuration.

User-tested in FS25 with AutoDrive 3.0.1.2. Lua 5.1 behavior checks pass. The
parking tool produced no Lua errors in the successful game session.

Known limitations: multiplayer has not been runtime-tested. Selecting the tool
can log warnings about missing optional clearing, indoor, AI and leveling areas
on its temporary catalog preview. No destination names or route connections
are created. This is a PC/Mac script companion, not a ModHub submission.
